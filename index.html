Static Squad – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Contact & support: veyraxis.studio@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Static Squad ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") handles user information on Android devices.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information, and the Game has no user accounts.

Playing the single-player modes (Survival, Campaign, Test Area) does not send any gameplay data off your device.

When you open the online multiplayer menu (Co-op, Duel 1v1 or Duel 3v3), the Game connects to Unity Gaming Services so that players can find each other and play together. For this the following is processed:

  • An anonymous player ID created by Unity Authentication. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
  • Room data: the room code, your selected pilot (for example "Spark"), whether you are ready, your ranked rating (Elo) and your matchmaking rating (a skill number the Game uses to match players fairly and to set the difficulty of computer-controlled opponents). Room data is sent to the devices of the other players in the same room.
  • Connection data: your IP address and network information, used by Unity Relay to connect players.
  • Gameplay data during a match: movement, attacks and match results exchanged between the players in the room.
  • Ranked Duel leaderboard: after a ranked Duel, your rating (Elo), your selected pilot and a player name generated by Unity (for example "SwiftFox#1234"; not your real name) are sent to Unity Leaderboards. The top of the leaderboard, including these names, is visible to other players in the Game.

There is one case where the Game connects without you opening the multiplayer menu: if the result of a ranked Duel could not be sent earlier (for example because you were offline), the Game connects to Unity Gaming Services the next time it starts and sends that result to the leaderboard. In that case your anonymous player ID, connection data and the leaderboard entry described above are processed at startup. If you have never played a ranked Duel, this does not happen.

Purchases. The Game offers optional purchases with real money (Gem packs, the premium Signal Pass and item bundles). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we never receive or store your card number or other payment details. To show prices and to deliver what you bought, the Game uses Unity In-App Purchasing, which connects to Google Play every time the Game starts, even if you never buy anything. According to Unity, this component processes:

  • Purchase history: which products were bought in the Game and the transaction identifiers of those purchases.
  • Identifiers: device or other identifiers and a user identifier. They do not include your name, email address or phone number.
  • Approximate location (such as your country or region). It is processed only for the moment it is needed and is not kept.
  • Diagnostic data: crash logs, diagnostics and performance data of the purchasing component.

2. Game Progress

Your progress (cores, gems, scrap, crates, pilots, upgrades, Signal Pass, missions, ranked rating and settings) is saved in a file on your device. Items and currency you buy are part of this progress, together with a short list of recent purchase transaction identifiers that keeps a purchase from being delivered twice. This file is not sent to us and we have no copy of it. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes this progress from your device, including items you bought.

The Game does not turn off Android's built-in backup. If device backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include the Game's saved data in your device backup, which is stored by Google under your own Google account, and may restore it when you reinstall the Game or set up a new device. We cannot access that backup. You can turn backup off or delete your device backup in your Android and Google account settings. See the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Information

The multiplayer data above is used only to create and join rooms, connect and match players, set the difficulty of computer-controlled opponents, run online matches and keep the ranked leaderboard. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Purchase data is used only to show prices, to complete and deliver purchases, to keep a purchase from being delivered twice and to keep the store working. As disclosed by Unity, the data processed by Unity In-App Purchasing is also used for analytics about how the purchasing component is used and how it performs. It is not used for advertising.

3a. Crash Reports

If the Game crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent to Unity Cloud Diagnostics so that we can fix the problem. A report contains the error and where it happened in the Game's code, the device model, operating system version and the Game version. It does not contain your name, email, contacts or location, and it is not used for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): Authentication, Lobby and Relay for online multiplayer, Leaderboards for the ranked Duel ladder, In-App Purchasing for the store and Cloud Diagnostics for crash reports. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play (Google): app distribution and payment processing (Google Play Billing). Your purchases appear in your Google Play order history. See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising, Analytics and Purchases

The Game shows no ads, contains no advertising SDKs and does not use the Advertising ID. It offers optional purchases with real money through Google Play (see section 1). The only analytics are the crash reports described above and the purchase, diagnostic and performance data processed by Unity In-App Purchasing. If ads are added in a future update, this policy will be updated before that update is released.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Multiplayer and purchase data is processed by Unity only to provide these services, and payments are processed by Google. Other players in your room see your selected pilot and your in-game actions, and their devices receive the room data listed in section 1 (including your ratings); players who open the ranked leaderboard see the names, ratings and pilots listed on it.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Room data exists only while the room is open.
  • The anonymous player ID is kept by Unity according to its privacy policy. Clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game removes it from your device and a new ID is created next time, unless Android restores the Game's data from your device backup (see section 2).
  • Ranked leaderboard entries (player name, rating and pilot) are kept while the leaderboard exists, unless you ask us to delete them.
  • Crash reports do not contain your name or an account and are kept by Unity Cloud Diagnostics under its own retention settings.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google in your Google Play order history under Google's own policies. We cannot see your payment details and cannot delete your order history.
  • Data processed by Unity In-App Purchasing is kept by Unity according to its privacy policy.

How to request deletion of your data (Static Squad, developer Metehan Hanedan)

  1. If you played ranked Duels, open the Game and go to Game Modes › Duel 1v1 › Leaderboard. Note the name shown under "Shown as".
  2. Email veyraxis.studio@gmail.com with the subject "Static Squad data deletion". Include that name if you have one. You do not need to send any other personal information.
  3. We delete your leaderboard entry and the data linked to your anonymous player ID in Unity Gaming Services within 30 days, and we reply to confirm.

What is deleted: the leaderboard entry (name, rating, pilot) and the Unity Authentication player record linked to it. What we cannot delete ourselves: your Google Play order history, which you manage in your Google account; anonymous crash reports that cannot be tied to you, which remain under Unity's retention settings; and data processed by Unity In-App Purchasing, for which we pass your request on to Unity where a deletion mechanism is available (you can also contact Unity directly as described in its privacy policy). Your game progress is stored on your device, not with us; you can delete it yourself at any time by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game. If Android device backup is on, a copy may also exist in your own Google account backup (see section 2), which you manage in your Google account settings.

9. Internet Connection

An internet connection is needed for online multiplayer, the ranked leaderboard and the store (prices and purchases). The single-player modes work offline.

10. Data Security

Connections to Unity Gaming Services and Google Play are encrypted in transit.
