Signal Lost – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Contact & support: metehanhanedan@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Signal Lost ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") handles user information on Android devices.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information, and the Game has no user accounts.

Single-player modes (Survival, Campaign, Test Area) do not send any data off your device.

When you open the online multiplayer menu (Co-op, Duel 1v1 or Duel 3v3), the Game connects to Unity Gaming Services so that players can find each other and play together. For this the following is processed:

  • An anonymous player ID created by Unity Authentication. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
  • Room data: the room code, your selected pilot (for example "Spark") and whether you are ready.
  • Connection data: your IP address and network information, used by Unity Relay to connect players.
  • Gameplay data during a match: movement, attacks and match results exchanged between the players in the room.
  • Ranked Duel leaderboard: after a ranked Duel, your rating (Elo), your selected pilot and a player name generated by Unity (for example "SwiftFox#1234"; not your real name) are sent to Unity Leaderboards. The top of the leaderboard, including these names, is visible to other players in the Game.

2. Game Progress

Your progress (cores, gems, pilots, upgrades, Signal Pass, missions and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes this progress.

3. How We Use Information

The multiplayer data above is used only to create and join rooms, connect players and run online matches. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

3a. Crash Reports

If the Game crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent to Unity Cloud Diagnostics so that we can fix the problem. A report contains the error and where it happened in the Game's code, the device model, operating system version and the Game version. It does not contain your name, email, contacts or location, and it is not used for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): Authentication, Lobby and Relay for online multiplayer, Leaderboards for the ranked Duel ladder and Cloud Diagnostics for crash reports. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play (Google): app distribution. See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising, Analytics and Purchases

The current version of the Game shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking SDKs (only the crash reports described above), does not use the Advertising ID and does not offer purchases with real money. If ads or real-money purchases are added in a future update, this policy will be updated before that update is released.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your information. Multiplayer data is processed by Unity only to provide the multiplayer service. Other players in your room see your selected pilot and your in-game actions; players who open the ranked leaderboard see the names, ratings and pilots listed on it.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Room data exists only while the room is open.
  • The anonymous player ID is kept by Unity according to its privacy policy. Clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game removes it from your device and a new ID is created next time.
  • Ranked leaderboard entries (player name, rating and pilot) are kept while the leaderboard exists, unless you ask us to delete them.
  • Crash reports do not contain your name or an account and are kept by Unity Cloud Diagnostics under its own retention settings.

How to request deletion of your data (Signal Lost, developer Metehan Hanedan)

  1. If you played ranked Duels, open the Game and go to Game Modes › Duel 1v1 › Leaderboard. Note the name shown under "Shown as".
  2. Email metehanhanedan@gmail.com with the subject "Signal Lost data deletion". Include that name if you have one. You do not need to send any other personal information.
  3. We delete your leaderboard entry and the data linked to your anonymous player ID in Unity Gaming Services within 30 days, and we reply to confirm.

What is deleted: the leaderboard entry (name, rating, pilot) and the Unity Authentication player record linked to it. What is kept: nothing linked to you; anonymous crash reports that cannot be tied to you remain under Unity's retention settings. Your game progress is stored only on your device; you can delete it yourself at any time by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game.

9. Internet Connection

An internet connection is needed only for online multiplayer.

10. Data Security

Connections to Unity Gaming Services are encrypted in transit.
